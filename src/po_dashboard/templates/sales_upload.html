Choose a CSV or Excel file

Export the sales report from QuickBooks Desktop (for example Sales by Customer Detail, with item, quantity, price and amount) as CSV or Excel and upload it. You will see how its columns were read, and what it would do, before anything is kept.

What the app does with it

  • Nothing is invented. A reference, quantity or price the export does not carry stays empty, and an invoice that cannot be read with certainty waits for a person.
  • Uploading the same file again adds nothing. An invoice that changed since it was accepted keeps its accepted figures and waits for review.
  • Credit memos, freight, tax and discounts are listed apart from goods and never change what was billed by themselves.
  • An invoice missing from a partial or old export is never called unbilled: the app needs the dates the export covers.
